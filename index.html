Painel do Criador — analytics for TikTok Shop creators

Painel do Criador (also the name of our TikTok for Business developer app) is a read-only analytics dashboard for TikTok Shop affiliate creators in Brazil. It puts each video's retention next to the sales it generated, so creators know which videos and products to scale. We started with our own accounts and are opening a closed beta for creators in our mentoring group.

  1. Each creator connects their own accountsThrough TikTok's official authorization screens. We never ask for TikTok passwords and never act on behalf of the account.
  2. Read-only insightsOnce a day we read the creator's own videos and organic insights (views, average time watched, full video watched rate, aggregated audience gender and country) through the TikTok API for Business.
  3. Joined with the creator's affiliate ordersWith a separate authorization, affiliate orders come from the TikTok Shop Partner Center API, so each video shows views, sales, commission and views per sale.
  4. Private and isolatedEach creator sees only their own data. Tokens are encrypted; data is never sold, shared or used for advertising; creators can delete everything at any time.
