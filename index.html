What we do

We work as a TikTok Shop affiliate creator, focused on product categories where a short, honest demonstration helps people decide.

01

Product research

We pick products from the TikTok Shop affiliate catalog based on demand, reviews and fit with each account's audience.

02

UGC video production

Scripts, recording and editing of 20–60 second videos that show the product in real use: supplements and fitness, sleep and home goods.

03

Testing and iteration

Every video is a test. We compare hooks and formats, keep what works and retire what does not, using our own performance data.

Ari Metricas — our in-house analytics

Ari Metricas is a private, internal tool used only by the owner of OAri Marketing. It brings together video performance and affiliate sales so we can see which videos actually drive results. It is not offered to third parties.

  1. Connect only our own accountsEach TikTok account we own is authorized by us through TikTok's official login and consent flow. No third-party accounts are connected.
  2. Read performance metrics, read-onlyOnce a day we read the list of our own videos and their metrics (views, likes, comments, shares, average watch time, completion rate and aggregated audience gender and country) through official TikTok APIs.
  3. Combine with our affiliate salesMetrics are matched by video ID with the affiliate sales report from our agency, so each video shows views, sales and revenue side by side.
  4. Store privatelyData is stored in a local database on our own computer, protected by a password and never shared, sold or used for advertising.
