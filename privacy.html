Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy explains how OAri Marketing (Ariel Batista da Silva, MEI, Brazil — "we") handles data in Ari Metricas and Ari Vendas, our internal analytics tools (together, "the tools"), and on this website.

1. Who uses the tools

The tools are used only by the owner of OAri Marketing. They are not public services and have no other users. The only TikTok and TikTok Shop creator accounts connected to them are accounts that we own and that we authorize ourselves through the official consent screens of TikTok and TikTok Shop Partner Center.

2. Data we access

  • Basic profile information of our own TikTok accounts (display name, username, avatar).
  • The list of our own videos and their metrics: views, likes, comments, shares, reach, average time watched, full video watched rate, and aggregated, anonymous audience breakdowns (gender and country percentages).
  • Affiliate orders of our own creator accounts, read through the TikTok Shop Partner Center API with read-only scopes (creator.affiliate_collaboration.read and creator.affiliate.info): order ID, date, status, product, quantity, order amount, commission and the video or live that generated the sale, plus our own affiliate profile.
  • Affiliate sales figures for our own videos, from the reports of our affiliate agency.

We do not request or store buyer personal data (names, addresses, phone numbers or e-mails); if any such field is returned by an API, it is discarded and never saved. We do not access data that identifies individual viewers, private messages, or any account we do not own. We do not post, comment, send messages or change anything on TikTok or TikTok Shop through the tools.

3. How we use it

Solely to measure the performance of our own content: which videos and products generate views and sales, so we can plan new videos. We do not sell, rent or share this data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored in a local database on a computer we control. Access tokens are kept in a private configuration file, never in source code, and are refreshed only through the official TikTok and TikTok Shop flows. The app secret is never placed in source code or on this website. The dashboard is protected by a password and is only reachable from our home network.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep metrics while they are useful for our analysis. We can revoke the tools' access at any time in TikTok's settings (Security and permissions → Manage app permissions) or in TikTok Shop's authorization settings; after revocation, no new data is collected and we delete the stored tokens. Stored metrics can be deleted on request.

6. This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts, and does not collect personal data. If you e-mail us, we use your message only to reply.

7. Your rights (LGPD)

Under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Lei 13.709/2018) you may ask us about any personal data we hold about you, and request correction or deletion.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: contato@oarimarketing.app.
