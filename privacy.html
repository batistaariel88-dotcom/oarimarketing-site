Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy explains how OAri Marketing (Ariel Batista da Silva, MEI, Brazil — "we") handles data in Ari Metricas, our internal analytics tool, and on this website.

1. Who uses Ari Metricas

Ari Metricas is used only by the owner of OAri Marketing. It is not a public service and has no other users. The only TikTok accounts connected to it are accounts that we own and that we authorize ourselves through TikTok's official consent screen.

2. Data we access

  • Basic profile information of our own TikTok accounts (display name, username, avatar).
  • The list of our own videos and their metrics: views, likes, comments, shares, reach, average time watched, full video watched rate, and aggregated, anonymous audience breakdowns (gender and country percentages).
  • Affiliate sales figures for our own videos, from the reports of our affiliate agency.

We do not access data that identifies individual viewers, private messages, or any account we do not own. We do not post, comment or change anything on TikTok through the tool.

3. How we use it

Solely to measure the performance of our own content: which videos and products generate views and sales, so we can plan new videos. We do not sell, rent or share this data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored in a local database on a computer we control. Access tokens are kept in a private configuration file, never in source code, and are refreshed only through TikTok's official flow. The dashboard is protected by a password and is only reachable from our home network.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep metrics while they are useful for our analysis. We can revoke the tool's access at any time in TikTok's settings (Security and permissions → Manage app permissions); after revocation, no new data is collected and we delete the stored tokens. Stored metrics can be deleted on request.

6. This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts, and does not collect personal data. If you e-mail us, we use your message only to reply.

7. Your rights (LGPD)

Under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Lei 13.709/2018) you may ask us about any personal data we hold about you, and request correction or deletion.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: contato@oarimarketing.app.
