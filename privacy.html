Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy explains how OAri Marketing (legal name 67.607.604 ARIEL BATISTA DA SILVA, a Brazilian sole proprietorship (MEI), CNPJ 67.607.604/0001-71, São Leopoldo/RS — "we") handles data in Painel do Criador, our analytics dashboard for TikTok Shop affiliate creators (the same name is used for our TikTok for Business developer app; on TikTok Shop Partner Center it is the custom app "Painel métricas"), and on this website. It follows Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018) and the Brazilian Internet Civil Framework (Law 12.965/2014).

1. Who uses Painel do Criador

TikTok Shop affiliate creators who create an account with us. Each creator connects only their own TikTok accounts through TikTok's official authorization screens. We never ask for TikTok passwords, and access can be revoked at any time.

2. Data we access and why

  • Account data: name, e-mail and a password stored only as a hash — to create and secure the creator's account (legal basis: performance of contract, LGPD art. 7, V).
  • Video insights of the creator's own TikTok Business account, read-only through the TikTok API for Business: video views, average time watched, full video watched rate and aggregated audience gender and country percentages — to show video performance (performance of contract).
  • Affiliate orders of the creator's own accounts, read-only through the TikTok Shop Partner Center API: order ID, date, status, product, quantity, amount, estimated commission and the video or live that generated the sale — to show sales and commission per video and product (performance of contract).
  • Access tokens for the connected accounts — stored encrypted, refreshed only through TikTok's official flows.
  • Access logs (IP address, date, time and action) — kept for 6 months as required by Law 12.965/2014, art. 15 (legal obligation).

We do not request or store buyer personal data (names, addresses, phone numbers or e-mails); if an API returns such fields, they are discarded. Audience data is aggregated by TikTok and does not identify individuals. We do not post, edit, delete, comment or send messages on TikTok; all permissions we use are read-only. We do not sell data, do not use it for advertising and do not build profiles.

3. Sharing

Data is shared only with: TikTok (the source of the data, which receives the authorization and our API requests); our hosting provider (stores the database); our e-mail provider (account e-mails such as password reset); and authorities when required by law. If a provider stores data outside Brazil, the transfer follows LGPD art. 33.

4. Security

HTTPS for all connections; passwords hashed with argon2; access tokens encrypted with a key kept outside the database; each creator can only see their own data (verified by automated tests); protection against forged forms, rate limiting of login attempts, and sessions invalidated on password change.

5. Retention and deletion

Account data, orders and insights are kept while the account exists and deleted when the creator deletes the account, which can be done at any time inside the dashboard. Disconnecting a TikTok account deletes its tokens. Access logs are kept for 6 months and then deleted automatically. Creators can also revoke our access in TikTok's own settings.

6. Your rights (LGPD, art. 18)

You may request confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymization, blocking or deletion, portability, information about sharing, and revocation of consent. Write to contato@oarimarketing.app; we reply within 15 days. You may also file a complaint with Brazil's data protection authority (ANPD).

7. Security incidents

If an incident may cause relevant risk or damage, we will notify affected creators and the ANPD (LGPD, art. 48), revoke the affected TikTok authorizations and rotate our credentials.

8. Cookies and this website

We use only strictly necessary cookies (session and form protection). This website has no analytics or tracking scripts.

9. Age

Painel do Criador is intended for people aged 18 or older, as required by the TikTok Shop affiliate program.
